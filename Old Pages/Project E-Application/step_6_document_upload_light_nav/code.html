Code of Conduct

NYC Film & Television Production Guidelines

1. Respect for Neighborhoods: Production companies must make every effort to minimize the disruption to neighbors. This includes strictly adhering to parking permit zones and noise ordinances.

2. Community Relations: A designated production representative must be available to address local resident and merchant concerns at all times during filming activity.

3. Environmental Responsibility: Productions are encouraged to use sustainable practices, including proper waste management and minimizing idling of production vehicles.

4. Safety Protocols: All activity must comply with New York State and NYC safety regulations. Failure to follow safety directives from NYPD or MOME officials may result in immediate permit revocation.

5. Use of Public Space: Public sidewalks and streets must remain accessible to the greatest extent possible. Pedestrian flow should not be blocked without explicit police supervision.
