What permits do I need to film in New York City?

expand_less

Most professional filming and photography on public property in New York City requires a permit from the Mayor's Office of Media and Entertainment (MOME).

  • Premiere Permit: For productions with significant equipment or parking needs.
  • Standard Permit: For most feature films, television shows, and commercials.
  • Student/Non-Profit: Reduced fee permits for educational or charitable projects.

Projects that do not interfere with pedestrian or vehicular traffic and use only hand-held equipment (camera and tripod) may not require a formal permit but are encouraged to register.
